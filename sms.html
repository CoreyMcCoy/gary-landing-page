SMS Program

Who sends the texts

Gary sends texts for local home service businesses, such as roofers, plumbers, HVAC, and electricians. Gary sends from a local number it manages for that business.

Why you got one

You called one of those businesses and nobody picked up. Gary texts you back within seconds so you don't have to call someone else. Gary only texts people who just called. There are no marketing messages, no blasts, and no lists. Landline callers aren't texted.

What the first text looks like

Hey, this is Ace Roofing. Sorry we missed you, we're on a job. What can we help with? Reply STOP to opt out.

If you reply, an AI assistant answers your questions and can book an appointment. If a person is needed, the business owner takes over.

The details

  • Frequency: Message frequency varies.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP. You'll get no more texts from that number.
  • Opt back in: Reply START.
  • Help: Reply HELP, or email hello@letgarybook.com.
