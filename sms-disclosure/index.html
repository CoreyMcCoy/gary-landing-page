How to opt in

End users opt in by calling first, then confirming by text. Corey McCoy publishes the Gary demo number, (254) 400-2952, on the SMS disclosure page (https://letgarybook.com/sms-disclosure/) as the phone number for the demo HVAC business Sweetgum Creek Heating & Air. When someone calls (254) 400-2952 and the call is missed, Gary by Corey McCoy sends exactly one text: "Hi, this is Gary by Corey McCoy for Sweetgum Creek Heating & Air. Sorry we missed your call at [time]! Want to continue by text? Reply YES to continue. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out." No further messages are sent unless the caller replies YES. If the caller ignores the text or replies STOP, nothing more is sent. After YES, the caller gets an opt-in confirmation, and messages are limited to their service request: answering questions, hours and pricing, booking a visit, and appointment confirmation.
