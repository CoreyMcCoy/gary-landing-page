How the Conversation Starts

Call (254) 400-2952 to try Gary's missed-call text-back demo. When your call is not answered, Gary may send an automated text to the number you called from to respond to that inquiry. You can reply to continue the demonstration. You can also start a conversation by texting that number yourself.

A call does not enroll you in recurring messages, promotions, or unrelated follow-ups. If you do not reply to the initial text, we do not send a series of reminders asking you to engage.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies with your conversation and any simulated appointment you request. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive texts is not required to purchase anything. You can contact us by email instead.

Stop Messages or Restart a Conversation

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation, and further texts will stop. Calling again does not cancel a previous STOP request. Reply START or UNSTOP if you want to receive texts again about the demo. Restarting texts does not enroll you in marketing messages.

Wrong Numbers and Help

If you receive a text but did not call or text Gary, you do not need to participate. Reply STOP to prevent further texts, or contact us so we can address the mistake. Reply HELP for help, email hello@letgarybook.com, or call (254) 400-2952.
