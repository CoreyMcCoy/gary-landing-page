Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Gary ("Gary," "we," "us") is a missed-call text-back and appointment booking service operated by sole proprietor Corey McCoy. Corey McCoy sends all Gary text messages, which are signed "Gary by Corey McCoy." This policy explains what we collect when you call us, text with us, or visit letgarybook.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Phone number and call/SMS metadata, such as the time of your call, caller ID name, and message delivery status, when you call a number Gary serves.
  • Text messages you send us, including photos, and the details you choose to share (like your name, address, what you need help with, and booking details such as the service, date, time, and email for your confirmation).
  • Your messaging consent record: when you replied YES, STOP, or START.
  • Website information: basic technical data your browser sends when you visit letgarybook.com, and anything you send to hello@letgarybook.com.

How we use it

  • To text you back after a missed call, but only if you agree by replying YES.
  • To answer your questions, share pricing and availability, and schedule the appointment you ask for.
  • To send messages about your request, like booking confirmations and updates.
  • To keep records of your consent and opt-out choices, and to keep the service secure and working.

We don't use your phone number or messages for marketing or promotional texts.

SMS / text messaging

When you call a number Gary serves (for the demo, (254) 400-2952) and the call is missed, Gary by Corey McCoy sends you exactly one text asking whether you want to continue by text. Texting starts only if you reply YES. After that, texts are about your request: answering questions, scheduling, and appointment updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, HELP for help, or START to opt back in. For help, email hello@letgarybook.com or call (254) 400-2952. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers and opt-in data are never sold to anyone. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the providers that deliver our text messages on our behalf.

To run the service, we use providers that process information only on our behalf and only to deliver Gary to you: our SMS provider and wireless carriers that deliver the texts, plus our hosting, database, AI text-processing, and calendar providers. They may not use your information for their own purposes. We share other information only if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of you, us, or others.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages and booking details for up to 24 months after your last conversation, and consent and opt-out records for as long as needed to honor your choices and meet legal and carrier record-keeping requirements. After that we delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see below).

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. No system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to stop all texts.
  • Email hello@letgarybook.com to ask what we hold about you or to ask us to delete it. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Agreeing to receive texts is never a condition of any purchase.

Children

Gary is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
