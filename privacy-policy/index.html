Privacy Policy – Gary

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Gary (“we,” “our,” “us”) provides missed-call text-back software for home-service businesses. This policy covers the Gary website and text demo, including information you provide when you call, text, or contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect the phone number associated with your call or text, call and message dates and identifiers, message content, and information you choose to provide during the conversation. This may include your name, address, service questions, and simulated appointment details. We also keep records of the inquiry that started the conversation and your messaging preferences, including opt-out and resubscription requests.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, run the missed-call demonstration, answer questions, walk through simulated scheduling, provide support, and honor your messaging preferences. Automated assistants process conversation information to provide these services. Information provided in the demo is used for the demonstration; it is not a request to dispatch a contractor.

Text Messages and Your Choices

When you call Gary's demo number and the call is not answered, Gary may send an automated text to respond to that inquiry. You can reply to continue the demo or send the first text yourself. Messages stay within your inquiry and any simulated appointment you request. A call does not enroll you in recurring marketing or unrelated messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help. Calling again does not override an opt-out. Reply START or UNSTOP to request texts again about the demo. Consent is not required to purchase anything. You can contact hello@letgarybook.com for help without using texts.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in and consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We use service providers for message delivery, automated assistance, data storage, and scheduling. They may process the information needed to provide those services on our behalf. SMS opt-in and consent information is not shared with third parties except messaging providers as needed to deliver messages and honor messaging preferences. We do not transfer your consent to another business or authorize providers to market to you.

Data Security and Retention

We use safeguards to protect stored information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. We retain information needed to provide the service, maintain inquiry and messaging preference records, and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of your information. We may retain records needed to honor an opt-out or meet legal obligations.
