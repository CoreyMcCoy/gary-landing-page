Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Who we are

Gary is run by Gary, Waco, Texas 76706. Gary is a missed-call text-back service for home service businesses. Questions: hello@letgarybook.com.

Site visitors

This site has no analytics, no tracking cookies, and no forms. It loads fonts from Google. The demo video loads from a third-party player only after you press play. Those providers may see your IP address when they load.

Client businesses

We collect what we need to set up and run Gary for you: business details, services, pricing, hours, service areas, the owner's contact info, and access to the Google Calendar you choose. Your operator sets this up. You don't log in to anything.

People who text with Gary

Gary texts you only after you call one of our clients and the call goes unanswered. If you reply, we collect your phone number and anything you send: your name, your address, your messages, and photos. If you book, we collect the appointment.

How we use it

Only to run the service for that client. That means texting you back, answering your questions, booking the job, and passing the conversation to the business owner. We don't send marketing texts and we don't keep marketing lists.

Who we share it with

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do share data with the providers that run the service, and only for that purpose:

  • Twilio: calls, texts, and phone number lookup
  • OpenAI: writing replies
  • Google: calendar
  • Clerk: operator sign-in
  • MongoDB: database hosting

Each conversation also goes to the client business it belongs to.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for 2 years.

Your data

Email hello@letgarybook.com to see your data or have it deleted. Texting STOP stops all further texts from that number.
